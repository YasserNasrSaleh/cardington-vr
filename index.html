Exterior finishes
Vision glazing, tinted double-glazed units Spandrel panels and roof parapet Aluminium mullions at 1.5 m, transoms, entrance canopy Rainscreen cladding over the stair cores on axes A and F Roof plant screen and lift overrun

An indicative envelope for a typical office building, set 450 mm outside the grid. It is not part of the Cardington test frame and is not counted in the member totals.

With a level selected, the envelope is cut away above that level.

Interior fit-out
Plasterboard partitions around the stair, toilet and lift cores Glazed meeting-room fronts and Level 1 balustrade Desk clusters of four with fabric screens Task and meeting chairs Lobby and breakout seating Carpet tiles; stone floor in the reception lobby Planters

A typical Category B office fit-out. Open-plan desking for up to 80 people per floor fills the 6.0 m bays along lines 1 to 2 and 3 to 4. Each side of the lift lobby has three glazed meeting rooms and a tea point. The ground floor has a reception lobby under the Level 1 void. The roof is not fitted out.

With a level selected, only that floor is furnished.

About this model

Grid 45.0 × 21.0 m: five 9.0 m bays (A–F) and three bays of 6.0, 9.0 and 6.0 m (1–4), with core gridlines A′, C′, E′, 2a and 2b.

Framing is taken from the four floor plans. Storey heights are set to 4.34 m at ground and 4.14 m above. X-bracing runs full height in the long direction on line 3 (A–A′, E′–F) and line 2b (C–D), and in the short direction on axes A and F between lines 2b and 3, and on axes C and D between lines 2 and 2b. Deck openings are indicative.
